Vikunja Tasks: widget design mockups

Visual ideas only, drawn in HTML. Nothing here is implemented in the plugin. Every tile uses Macro Deck deck-cell proportions (120 px cell, 12 px gap) and lengths relative to the tile's smaller side, like the real widget does.

Sizes shown: 1x1, 2x1, 1x2, 2x2 and 4x2. "Read only" tiles have nothing to press. "Checklist" tiles show what pressing does.
